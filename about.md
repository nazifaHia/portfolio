# About Me

I am a Scientist at the Centre for Advanced Research in Sciences (CARS), University of Dhaka. My work combines machine learning research with practical applications in embedded systems and software engineering.

I manage the IoT and Robotics Lab, where I supervise postgraduate research, support student-led projects, and help develop research infrastructure for Edge AI, embedded systems, and intelligent computing.

Previously, I served as a Lecturer at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB), teaching undergraduate courses across Cyber Security, Computer Networks, Web Technologies, Java, .NET, and Database Systems. Before entering academia, I worked as a Software Engineer at Orion Informatics Ltd., contributing to enterprise financial software used by more than 15 financial institutions and over 200,000 registered users.

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on:
